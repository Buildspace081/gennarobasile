## Why this course matters

Text Mining connects statistical language processing with the systems behind modern language models. It is a foundation for understanding—not merely using—embeddings, transformers, retrieval and agents.

## Learning path

1. Foundation models and next-token prediction
2. Encoders, decoders and sequence representations
3. Self-attention and transformers
4. Retrieval-augmented generation
5. Tool calling and agents
